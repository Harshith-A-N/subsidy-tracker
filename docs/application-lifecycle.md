# Application Lifecycle

Every subsidy application moves through predefined stages.

## Lifecycle

APPLICATION_SUBMITTED
    ↓
ELIGIBILITY_PENDING
    ↓
ELIGIBLE
    ↓
FIELD_VERIFICATION_PENDING
    ↓
FIELD_APPROVED
    ↓
DISTRICT_REVIEW_PENDING
    ↓
DISTRICT_APPROVED
    ↓
FINANCE_REVIEW_PENDING
    ↓
FINANCE_APPROVED
    ↓
READY_FOR_DISBURSEMENT
    ↓
DISBURSED
    ↓
COMPLETED

---

FIELD_REJECTED
DISTRICT_REJECTED
FINANCE_REJECTED
RE_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED
APPLICATION_CANCELLED
